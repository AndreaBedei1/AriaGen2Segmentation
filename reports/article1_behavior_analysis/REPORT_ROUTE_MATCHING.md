## Privacy

No file in this directory contains a latitude or a longitude. Positions are
relative metres from the track origin, both ends of each track are trimmed by
250 m of travelled distance, and the raw Overpass cache — which does contain
absolute coordinates — lives under `output/` and is not committed.
`tests/test_behavior_guards.py` fails the build if a coordinate column appears in
any committed CSV.

## Matching method

A Viterbi decode over candidate road positions (Newson & Krumm), not a per-point
nearest-road snap. With a median reported GPS accuracy of 27.5 m the car's fixes
are wider than the gap between parallel carriageways, and independent snapping
flips between them. The emission is perpendicular distance scaled by the
reported accuracy; the transition compares the distance travelled against the
distance along the road.

A sample that fails the 30 m snap gate or the 60° heading gate is returned
**unmatched with its reason**, not attached to the nearest road anyway. Heading
gating is suspended below 2 m/s, where GPS heading is noise.

## Spatial binning

Bins are defined by the map — `(osm_way_id, floor(offset_along_way / bin_size),
travel_direction)` — so a bin is the same place for both recordings and the
pairing does not depend on which vehicle is treated as the reference. The two
drives are aligned **in space only**; they happened weeks apart and share nothing
but the road.

## The GPS cadence, not the configuration, sets the usable bin size

At 1 Hz and 10–15 m/s a fix lands every 10–15 m. A bin narrower than that cannot
hold the two fixes the pairing rule requires, so a low paired count at 10 m is a
sampling limit and not a statement about the routes.

| bin size | shared bins | paired | paired length | expected fixes/bin | undersampled |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 10 m | 122 | 1 | 10 m | 0.7 | **yes** |
| 20 m | 109 | 28 | 560 m | 1.3 | **yes** |
| 50 m | 52 | 42 | 2 100 m | 3.3 | no |

The analysis bin is therefore **50 m** — the smallest configured size the cadence
can actually fill. The 10 m and 20 m rows are reported with their caveat rather
than suppressed, because the reason they are small is itself a result.

## Frame-level bin assignment

Frames reach a bin through their **nearest real GPS fix**; no position is
invented between fixes, and the positional uncertainty that choice implies is
carried on every row.
