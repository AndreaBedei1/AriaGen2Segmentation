## Three findings that constrain everything downstream

### 1. No usable VIO/SLAM pose stream

Streams `502-1` and `504-1` are present but carry 12 and 1 records and fail to
activate; `503-1` and `505-1` are empty. There is no device pose.

Consequence: **vehicle speed cannot come from pose** and falls back to the GPS
receiver's own speed field. This is recorded on every row of
`vehicle_dynamics.parquet` as `speed_source`, and it is the single largest
limitation on the dynamics results.

### 2. GPS quality differs sharply between the two recordings

| | car | motorcycle |
|---|---:|---:|
| GPS records | 374 | 1 004 |
| with coordinates at all | 246 (65.8%) | 953 (94.9%) |
| passing the accuracy gate | 219 (58.6%) | 953 (94.9%) |
| median reported accuracy | 27.5 m | 3.0 m |
| 90th percentile accuracy | 94.0 m | 5.0 m |

128 of the car's 374 GPS records carry no position at all. The car recording is
an urban drive that appears to have suffered poor sky visibility; the motorcycle
recording is nearly clean. This asymmetry propagates into map matching, route
binning and the lateral-displacement analysis, and is the reason several results
are graded lower for the car than for the motorcycle.

### 3. `temperature` is a device sensor, not a body temperature

The stream interleaves 25 named sensors — `TEMP_NOSE_PAD`,
`TEMP_LEFT_TEMPLE_OUTER`, `TEMP_SOC_VIRTUAL_FRONT_INNER` and so on — each at
1 Hz. A naive read produces a sawtooth that is an artefact of the interleaving,
so the reader de-interleaves by sensor name and carries the temporal distance for
every non-reference sensor.

Nothing here is validated as a body temperature. It is labelled
`device_or_environment_temperature_context` throughout and no physiological
inference is drawn from it.

## Hand tracking

| | car | motorcycle |
|---|---:|---:|
| left hand tracked | 96.5% | 31.7% |
| right hand tracked | 99.0% | 12.1% |

This is **tracker output, not hand visibility**. A rider's hands are on the bars,
low and wide, and largely outside the tracking volume of a head-mounted system;
they were plainly visible in the image for much of the recording. The difference
must not be reported as "the motorcyclist's hands were less visible".
